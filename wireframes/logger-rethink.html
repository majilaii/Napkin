Napkin — Logger Rethink · v0

From form to page. The composer should feel like writing, not like filing.

Three concepts exploring how to make solo logging feel like a journaling ritual instead of a rating workflow. The rating survives, but it sits beneath the writing — a by-product of the act, not the act itself.

Concept A
the page
Restaurant name becomes an italic masthead. The writing surface is the hero — 60% of the screen, serif prose, no field chrome. Rating sits quietly beneath. Dish, companions, breakdown, table all collapse into a single add details drawer.

Concept C
the day logger
Instead of "what did you eat right now," the + action opens on today as a day-page. Swipe back through days to catch up on meals you didn't log in the moment. This is the retroactive journaling use case — sit down Sunday morning and log the week.

What shifts

  • Writing surface is the hero, not a field
  • Rating is a by-product, shown as a serif numeral
  • Dish / breakdown / Table → one "add details" drawer
  • Solo users never see Table chrome on day one
  • Date & meal-slot are first-class (backfill)

What stays

  • 5-star rating (hidden but present)
  • Secondary breakdown ratings — as drawer option
  • Companion tagging (the Moment-2 primitive)
  • Table share — but only as a quiet footer
  • Photo upload flow untouched

Open questions

  • Does the day-logger replace the + action, or sit beside it?
  • Should the composer default to the last-eaten-at time, or "now"?
  • Pull-quote extraction: first sentence, or user-selected line?
  • How does "your second visit · last time 3.5" feel if rating drops?
  • Is "Noted" still the right verb for solo, or something quieter?
