Atlas · Wireframes v2

A table's map is its travel journal.

Atlas is a geographic lens on the places a Table has already been — city-first, grid by default, map as a toggle. It aggregates per restaurant: solo visits, rounds, and mixed histories all collapse to one pin. Lives alongside Activity and Wishlist; cross-links back into the restaurant page; quietly fills with every new log.

Open Questions

Three things left to resolve.

  1. Round / solo aggregation precedence
    If a restaurant has 1 round + 4 solos, does the tile's primary rating show the round's group-avg or a blended average of all visits? The number on the tile sets the sort order for "top rated," so this is load-bearing. Lean: group-avg of the round, with solos stacked underneath on the peek sheet. Rounds are the signal; solos are the trail.
  2. Wishlist-to-atlas notification
    When Julian logs a spot you had wishlisted, do you get a soft tick in your feed — or is the quiet heart-mark on the tile the only acknowledgement? In-feed is louder but potentially more satisfying. Lean: yes, one soft feed tick ("Julian went to Fu He Hui — you had this saved"). Ship as a follow-up ticket; not blocking Atlas v1.
  3. City-sort default on the index
    Most-logged first (weight by activity), most-recent (active cities rise), or alphabetical (calm, unopinionated)? Affects what Shanghai-vs-Tokyo-vs-London users see at top on open. Lean: most-recent. The Table's rhythm comes forward; dormant chapters settle.
  4. Pin rating on mixed
    Show Round's group-avg OR a blended avg on mixed pins (Round + later solos)? Sets the number people read first on the map. Lean: Round's group-avg. Rounds are the event; solos annotate.
