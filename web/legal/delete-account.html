Delete your account

napkin · built by Jacky Ieong

You can delete your Napkin account and all data associated with it at any time — from inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app installed.

From inside the app

Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Without the app

Email jackyieong@mainrichinternational.com with the subject "Delete my account", sent from the email address on your Napkin account (so we can verify it's you). We'll confirm and delete the account within 30 days — usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Everything. Your account and profile, logs and reviews, photos, saved spots and lists, comments and reactions, and your Table memberships are all permanently removed. No data is kept afterwards, and deletion cannot be undone.
