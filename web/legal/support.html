frequently asked

How do I import spots from a video link?

Share the post to Napkin from TikTok or Instagram, or open Places in Napkin and tap the import button (the downward arrow) to paste a TikTok, Instagram, Google Maps or restaurant link, or to pick a screenshot or a saved video. The first time, Napkin asks before anything is sent to Anthropic, which reads the post to find the spots. Napkin suggests the spots it found, you approve the ones you want, and they are pinned to your Places.

Are my logs public?

Profiles are public by default, and while yours is public, the places you log, your ratings and your reviews show on it. A log with a written review also appears on that restaurant's page. Make your profile private in Settings → Privacy and your logs are visible only to you, your Tables and the friends you tag. Tables themselves are never public.

What is a Table?

A small private group — you and the people you actually eat with. Tablemates see what's shared to the Table, and everyone's saved spots overlap into a shared wishlist ("3 of you want to try this"). Invite-only, never public.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → delete account (at the bottom of Settings). It's immediate and irreversible: logs, photos, wishlist, comments, and Table memberships are all deleted. Locked out? Email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report content or block someone?

Report any post or user from inside the app, and block users from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks the rules is removed, and offending accounts can be terminated.

How do I reset my password?

Tap forgot password? on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link. Nothing after a few minutes? Check spam, then email us.
