frequently asked

How do I import spots from a video link?

Copy the link, tap the + button in Napkin, and choose add from link — you can paste a TikTok, Maps, or restaurant URL. Video links are read entirely on your device; nothing is uploaded. Napkin suggests the spots it found, you approve the ones you want, and they land on your wishlist.

Are my logs public?

Logs are private by default. A log can appear on your public profile only if it has a written review and your profile is public. Profiles are public by default — make yours private in Settings → Privacy. Tables are never public.

What is a Table?

A small private group — you and the people you actually eat with. Tablemates see what's shared to the Table, and everyone's saved spots overlap into a shared wishlist ("3 of you want to try this"). Invite-only, never public.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. It's immediate and irreversible: logs, photos, wishlist, comments, and Table memberships are all deleted. Locked out? Email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I report content or block someone?

Report any post or user from inside the app, and block users from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours; content that breaks the rules is removed, and offending accounts can be terminated.

How do I reset my password?

Tap forgot password? on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link. Nothing after a few minutes? Check spam, then email us.
