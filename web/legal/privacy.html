Privacy Policy

Effective 16 July 2026 · built and operated by Jacky Ieong

Napkin is a meal journal for iPhone: you log the restaurants you eat at, keep a wishlist of ones to try, and share — if you choose — with small private groups called Tables. This page explains what data Napkin handles and what happens to it. The short version: your data exists to power your journal. No ads, no data sales, no tracking.

1.What we collect

  • Account. Your email address and password, handled by Supabase Auth (passwords are stored hashed — we never see them), plus your display name and, for new accounts, a profile photo.
  • Things you make. Restaurant logs — ratings, written notes, dates, tagged companions — the photos you attach, wishlist saves, lists, your Table memberships and activity, comments, and emoji reactions.
  • Location — optional, foreground only. If you allow it, your coordinates are used to find restaurants near you and to sort saved spots by distance. They serve the search in front of you and nothing else: we don't record where you've been or build a location history. Restaurant coordinates come from Google, not from tracking you.
  • Search text. What you type into restaurant search is sent to Google Places to return results.
  • Imported video text. Paste a TikTok or similar link and the video is read on your device — frames and audio are turned into text locally. Only that extracted text (restaurant names, captions) is sent to our server and to Anthropic's Claude to identify the restaurants. The video itself is never uploaded, stored, or redistributed.
  • Usage events. First-party events like "logged a meal", tied to your account id and stored in our own database. There is no third-party analytics SDK in the app.

2.How we use it

  • To run the app: your journal, feed, wishlist, Tables, and profile.
  • To show what you make to the people you chose — Tablemates, tagged friends, or anyone, if your profile is public.
  • To find restaurants: search text (and, for nearby search, transient coordinates) goes to Google Places; imported video text goes to Anthropic.
  • To keep user images safe: before an avatar or meal photo is published, a standardized JPEG copy is sent to Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch to check for adult, violent, or sexually suggestive content. Rejected images are not published.
  • To see which features get used, via our own usage events.
  • To email you about your account — password resets, replies when you write to us.

We never sell your data, never show ads, and never share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3.What's public, what's private

  • Profiles are public by default. Yours can be found and browsed unless you switch it off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Logs are private by default. A log can appear on your public profile only if it has a written review and your profile is public. Ratings-only logs never appear publicly.
Tables are never public. Anything shared to a Table is visible only to that Table's members, whatever your profile setting.

5.How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Your search text and location aren't stored as a history — they serve the request in front of you, and that's it. When you delete your account, your data goes with it.

6.Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account. The request immediately freezes new uploads and signs you out. Your logs, photos, wishlist, comments, Table memberships, and account are then deleted irreversibly; storage cleanup is recorded durably and retries if a provider call fails. If you'd rather we handle it — or you can't get into the app — email jackyieong@mainrichinternational.com.

7.Your rights

Wherever you live, you get the GDPR-style basics:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — fix your account data (most of it is editable in the app).
  • Deletion — see section 6.
  • Objection and restriction — ask us to stop or limit a particular use.
  • Portability — ask for your content in a machine-readable format.

Email jackyieong@mainrichinternational.com and we'll respond within a month. Data is hosted in the EU (see section 4); if you're in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8.Children

Napkin is for people aged 13 and over — or older, if that's the minimum age where you live. We don't knowingly collect data from children below that age; if you believe a child has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

9.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. Meaningful changes get flagged in the app.

10.Contact

Napkin is built and run by one person — Jacky Ieong. Email jackyieong@mainrichinternational.com and you'll be writing to the developer, not a ticket queue.
